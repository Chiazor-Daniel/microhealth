# Blood Pressure

## What it measures
Blood pressure shows the force of blood against artery walls. It is written as systolic/diastolic, e.g., 118/76 mmHg.

## Typical adult ranges
- Normal: systolic below 120 and diastolic below 80
- Elevated: systolic 120–129 and diastolic below 80
- High: systolic 130+ or diastolic 80+

## When to seek care
- Very high readings such as 180/120 or above require urgent attention.
- Persistent readings above 140/90 should be discussed with a clinician.
- Very low readings with dizziness or fainting need prompt evaluation.

## MicroHealth guidance
A single reading is rarely enough to diagnose a problem. Trend matters. Take readings at rest, seated, with correct cuff placement.
