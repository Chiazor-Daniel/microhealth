# Body Temperature

## What it measures
Body temperature reflects your body's ability to generate and get rid of heat.

## Typical range
Most adults: about 36.1–37.2°C.

## Fever guidance
- 37.3–38.0°C: low-grade fever. Rest, hydrate, monitor.
- 38.1–39.0°C: fever. Monitor symptoms; contact clinician if persistent or severe.
- Above 39.0°C: high fever; seek medical guidance, especially with confusion, stiff neck, breathing difficulty, or rash.

## MicroHealth guidance
Temperature varies during the day. Take readings at the same time daily when trending.
