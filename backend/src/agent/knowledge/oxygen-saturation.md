# Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂)

## What it measures
SpO₂ estimates the percentage of oxygen-carrying haemoglobin in the blood.

## Typical range
Most healthy adults: 95–100%.

## When to be cautious
- 92–94%: lower than usual; consider rest, deep breathing, and rechecking.
- Below 92%: contact a clinician promptly, especially with shortness of breath.
- Below 90%: seek urgent care.

## MicroHealth guidance
Cold fingers, nail polish, poor circulation, or movement can affect readings. Warm hands and stay still during measurement.
