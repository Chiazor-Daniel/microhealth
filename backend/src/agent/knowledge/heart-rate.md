# Heart Rate

## What it measures
Heart rate is the number of times your heart beats per minute (BPM). It reflects how hard your heart is working.

## Typical resting range
Most adults: 60–100 BPM at rest.
Athletes or very fit people may have lower resting heart rates.

## What affects it
Activity, stress, anxiety, caffeine, medications, illness, sleep, dehydration, and fitness level.

## When to be cautious
Seek urgent care if resting heart rate is persistently:
- Above 120 BPM, or
- Below 50 BPM with symptoms such as dizziness, fainting, chest pain, or trouble breathing.

## MicroHealth guidance
A gradual rise in resting heart rate over several days can be worth watching, especially if accompanied by other symptoms. Hydration, rest, and monitoring help.
